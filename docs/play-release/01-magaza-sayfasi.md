ikonu bile sana küsüyor!

## Short description (EN)
Place letters, claim the board, beat your rival — with a cute mascot by your side.

## Full description (EN)
Kelime Tahtı (Word Throne) brings the classic word-board game to live, fair competition.
• Main game: build words on the board, hit bonus squares, take the board.
• Last Letter duel: start your word with your rival's last letter — 15 seconds a turn.
• Word Workshop: finish 3 tasks with 7 letters in 60 seconds.
• Mascots that react, give hints and even sulk on your home screen if you stay away.
• Weekly Cup, leagues, friends and clubs. Store items are cosmetic only — no pay-to-win.
